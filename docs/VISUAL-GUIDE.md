# Burnsville Visual Guide

## PRODUCT ARTWORK

- Approved product cards are authoritative for Home and Shop discovery surfaces.
- Approved transparent bottle assets are authoritative for bottle-led product presentation.
- Product artwork must not be regenerated, recoloured, rewritten, distorted or replaced without explicit approval.

## PRODUCT SURFACES

### Home

- Use approved product cards for product discovery.
- Preserve clear hierarchy, responsive spacing and direct paths into Shop and Product Detail.

### Shop

- Use `cardImage || image`.
- Maintain consistent card dimensions, product identity and heat-level presentation.

### Product Detail

- Bottle asset is primary.
- Full product card is secondary supporting artwork.
- Product name, identifier, heat level and approved copy must match the product authority.

## GLOBAL UI

- Responsive across desktop, tablet and mobile.
- Navigation must remain clear and keyboard-accessible.
- Loading, empty and error states must be explicit.
- Checkout and account flows must remain visually consistent with the current application.

## CHANGE CONTROL

Visual changes require explicit approval and must not alter approved product identities or product artwork.
